My First Day

Today was the beginning of a new chapter in my life—my first day in college. I woke up earlier than usual because I didn’t want to be late. While getting ready, I felt a mix of excitement and nervousness. My mind was full of questions. Would I make new friends? Would my professors be strict? Would I be able to handle everything? When I arrived at the campus, I noticed how big and different it was compared to my old school. There were so many students walking around, and for a moment, I felt small and out of place. As I entered my classroom, my heart was beating fast. I quietly chose a seat and looked around. Some students were already talking and laughing, while others looked just as nervous as me. Our professor introduced themselves and explained what we would expect in the subject. As the day went on, I slowly started to feel more comfortable. I even talked to a few classmates, and it made me feel relieved knowing I wasn’t alone in feeling nervous. By the time I went home, I felt proud of myself. Even though I was nervous, I made it through my first day. I realized that college might be scary at first, but it is also full of new opportunities and experiences. I am excited to see what the next days will bring.

Exam Week

This week was one of the most stressful weeks I have experienced in college so far because it was exam week. Every day, I felt pressure because I knew I had to do well. I spent most of my time reviewing my notes, reading my lessons again, and trying to remember everything my professors taught. Sometimes I felt tired and overwhelmed, especially when I realized how many topics I needed to study. There were moments when I doubted myself and wondered if I studied enough. I also noticed that my classmates felt the same way. Some of them looked serious and quiet, while others talked about how nervous they were. Even though it was stressful, it also motivated me to do my best. I learned how to manage my time better and focus on my priorities. I sacrificed some of my free time so I could prepare more. When I finally finished my exams, I felt a huge sense of relief. All the stress and pressure slowly disappeared. I realized that exam week was not only about testing what I learned but also about teaching me responsibility, patience, and hard work. Even though it was difficult, I am proud of myself for getting through it. It showed me that I am stronger and more capable than I thought..
